# Data analysis workflow

1. Clarify the unit of observation, target question, time window, population and available fields.
2. Inspect shape, types, missingness, duplicates, ranges, categorical cardinality and likely leakage.
3. Define metric formulas and denominators before interpreting results.
4. Prefer simple baselines and descriptive analysis before complex models.
5. Separate correlation from causation; explain sample bias and uncertainty.
6. Make transformations reproducible and preserve raw data. Never fabricate measurements or fill missing values silently.
7. Deliver findings with a short method, evidence, caveats and next experiment.
