---
name: debugging
description: Diagnose runtime errors, exceptions and failing tests.
triggers: error, exception, traceback, bug, failing, failure, 401, 403, 404, 429, crash, not working
---
Debug from evidence. Identify the exact failing layer, reproduce the issue when possible, explain the root cause, make the smallest fix, and add a regression test. Do not hide errors or report success without running the relevant check.
