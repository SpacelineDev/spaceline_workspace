# Presentation analysis

1. Map the deck's narrative in slide order: audience, objective, setup, evidence, recommendation and close.
2. Summarize each slide and identify missing transitions, repeated content and unsupported claims.
3. Assess message clarity, information density, visual hierarchy and accessibility from available text only.
4. Flag slides whose meaning depends on images, charts, diagrams, speaker notes or animation not present in the extract.
5. Recommend a specific revised storyline and prioritized slide-level changes.
6. Never claim to have visually reviewed slide artwork unless screenshots/images were actually supplied and inspected.
